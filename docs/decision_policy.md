## Approved choices

### Comment-only change

A parser-verified COMMENT_ONLY change with no contract-bearing marker maps to NO_ASSURANCE_IMPACT for the narrow change finding. This is a syntax classification, not a claim of behavioral equivalence. Contract-bearing markers (must, always, never, precondition, invariant, default, returns, raises, deprecated) trigger review; a marker alone does not establish normative authority or a violation.

### Contract authority hierarchy

Use: explicit normative requirement > approved API/schema contract > explicitly designated specification > binding tests > documentation with established normative status > implementation behavior > inferred assumptions. Lower-ranked applicable sources do not override higher-ranked applicable sources. Same-level conflicts remain unresolved without a separate approved conflict rule.

### Test obligation

Use a separate test_obligation axis. MISSING_TEST is not a traceability or consistency label. An explicit obligation may come from an applicable normative requirement, an approved verification registry, or an approved acceptance criterion. UNSATISFIED requires a complete, revision-correct search that establishes no qualifying test. Otherwise preserve UNVERIFIED. Test count/path similarity alone does not establish coverage.

### 95% acceptance

The primary criterion is an observed point estimate of at least 95% on a frozen, independently adjudicated cohort. Report trace, consistency, test-obligation, evidence validity, and end-to-end metrics separately where applicable; the project's mandatory 95% objective continues to apply to all predeclared applicable quality gates. Report sample sizes, class/project/change-family breakdowns, intervals, coverage and abstention. The 12 authored cases are development/regression only and cannot establish acceptance. Independent performance remains NOT_ESTABLISHED until a fresh independent cohort and judgments exist.

## Authority and uncertainty

Every derived fact must be backed by revision-scoped source provenance. Unknown/missing extraction is not absence. Graph connections and route candidates identify investigation targets, not causality. A decision requiring normative authority remains uncertain or potential when the authority search is incomplete. Model-reported confidence is retained as raw provenance only and is not calibrated probability.
