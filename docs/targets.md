# Evaluation targets

- Development diagnostic target: 33/36 (91.67%) strict consistency agreement, status **NOT MEASURED** for the proposed frozen 36-case cohort. The original 12 are an exposed regression subset; up to 24 additional cases require provenance and human review. A combined result is a development result.
- Separate research target: at least 95% observed agreement on a frozen, independently adjudicated, repository-family-held-out cohort. Status **NOT ESTABLISHED**.
- Which target governs reporting: **BOTH, tracked separately**. The 33/36 goal is a development diagnostic target; the >=95% goal is the separate independently adjudicated research target. The principal scientific claim uses the independent target; a development result cannot substitute for it.
- 33/36 has an approximate Wilson 95% interval of 78.2%–97.1%; 35/36 is 97.22%. Correlated cases require cluster-aware analysis. The target must not influence labels, rules, prompts, case selection or exclusions.
- Strict agreement counts an abstention against a decisive gold label as incorrect. A smaller evaluated denominator is reported as k/n and cannot establish the 33/36 target.
